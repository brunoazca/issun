How it works

From full storage to
free space in four steps.

No settings to study and no file formats to pick. Issun shows you where the space went and handles the rest.

STEP 01

See where your storage went.

Issun lists every video in your library sorted by size, so the space hogs are right at the top. Select the ones you want to shrink.

STEP 02

Choose the quality and see the savings.

Pick a level from Small to Maximum for all videos at once, or fine-tune each one. You see the estimated size before you start.

SmallBalancedHighMaximum
STEP 03

Compress. Nothing lost but space.

Compressed copies go back into your library with the original date and location, so your timeline stays exactly the same. HDR, orientation and audio are kept too.

STEP 04

Compare, then clean up.

Play the original and the compressed version and switch between them. When you're happy, delete the originals in one step, and Issun walks you through emptying Recently Deleted to get the space back.

Support

Questions, answered.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?
No. All compression happens on your iPhone. Your videos never leave your device.
Will my videos look worse?
Issun re-encodes to HEVC and shows an estimated visual quality before you start. You choose the level, from Small to Maximum. Afterwards you can play the compressed copy and the original to compare them before deleting anything.
Do I lose the date and location?
No. Compressed copies are saved to your library with the original creation date and location, so they stay in the same place in your timeline.
Where did my storage go after deleting originals?
Deleted videos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. Empty it to get the space back. Issun walks you through it.
Can I undo a deletion?
Yes. Originals stay in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days and can be restored from there.
Why is there an ad?
A short video ad plays while each batch compresses. If no ad is available, compression continues without one.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Background compression with a system progress indicator needs iOS 26.

Still stuck? Questions, bugs or feedback: issunapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
