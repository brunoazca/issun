Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

The short version

  • Your videos are compressed on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Issun has no accounts and no servers of its own.
  • Issun sends anonymous usage events (counts, sizes and outcomes, never your videos) to PostHog so we can see how the app is used.
  • Issun shows a video ad from Google AdMob while your videos compress. Google collects some device data to serve ads, subject to your consent and tracking choices.

Issun: Video Compressor ("Issun", "we") is an iPhone app by Bruno Azambuja Carvalho. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Your videos and Photos library

With your permission, Issun reads your Photos library to list your videos by size. It compresses only the videos you choose, and saves the compressed copies back to your library with their original date and location. If you ask it to, Issun deletes the original videos. iOS asks you to confirm first, and the originals go to Recently Deleted.

All of this happens on your device. Issun does not send your videos, photos, file names, thumbnails or their metadata (such as dates or locations) anywhere.

You can change Photos access anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Data stored on your device

Issun keeps a few values on your iPhone to power your profile and ranks: the number of videos and batches compressed, the space freed, the last rank you've seen and whether you've shared your savings card. These values are not sent to us and are removed when you delete the app.

Sharing your savings card

From your profile you can share an image of how much space you've freed. It opens the standard iOS share sheet, and the image goes only where you choose to send it.

Anonymous usage analytics (PostHog)

To understand how the app is used and improve it, Issun sends anonymous events to PostHog, an analytics service. Events are sent to PostHog's US servers.

What is sent

EventDetails included
Photos access answeredWhether you allowed full, limited or no access
Compression set up and startedNumber of videos, their total size and the average quality level chosen
Ad finishedWhether the ad was completed, skipped or unavailable
Compression finishedHow many videos were done, skipped, failed or cancelled, sizes before and after, how long it took, and whether it finished in the background
Originals deletedNumber of videos and space freed
Profile openedNone
App lifecycleApp installed, updated, opened or sent to the background

Like most analytics tools, the PostHog SDK also attaches basic technical information to each event, such as app version, iOS version, device model, language and time zone. It uses a random identifier that it generates and stores on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, Apple ID, email or advertising identifier. When the app connects to PostHog, PostHog receives your IP address and may use it to estimate an approximate location (such as country or city).

Issun does not use PostHog for advertising. It does not create user profiles there, and it does not record your screen or sessions.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you start compressing, Issun plays a rewarded video ad from Google AdMob. If no ad is available, compression continues without one.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information,
  • your interactions with ads,
  • crash and performance diagnostics.

Google collects and processes this data, not Issun. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Brazil and other regions): where the law requires it (for example the GDPR or LGPD), Issun shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can decline, and you can change your choice later.
  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Issun may track you for more relevant ads. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not used, and the app works exactly the same. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Photos access: you can grant full or limited access, or revoke it, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.
  • Analytics: there is no in-app switch for analytics. Because events are anonymous, we usually can't link them to you, but you can still email us with any request.

What we don't do

We don't upload your videos, don't run servers that receive your personal data, don't require an account and don't sell your data.

Children

Issun is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version on this page with a new date.
