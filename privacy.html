Issun Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Issun is a video compressor for iPhone. Your videos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Your videos and Photos library

With your permission, Issun reads your Photos library to list your videos by size, compresses the videos you choose, and saves the compressed copies back to your library with their original date and location. If you ask it to, Issun moves the original videos to Recently Deleted. All of this happens on your device. Issun does not send your videos, photos, or their metadata anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Issun keeps a local count of videos compressed and space freed. It stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising

Issun shows a rewarded video ad provided by Google AdMob while your videos compress. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by Issun. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks whether Issun may track you for personalized ads. If you decline, ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Brazil and other regions): where required by law (such as the GDPR or LGPD), Issun shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can decline, and you can change your choice later.

What we don't do

Issun has no accounts, no analytics of its own, and no servers. We do not sell your data, and we do not receive your videos or personal information.

Children

Issun is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
